Questions

Frequently asked
questions.

How long does a typical project take?
Most websites take between 1–3 weeks depending on scope, from first call to launch. E-commerce and larger builds can take longer — we'll give you a clear timeline before we start.
Do you work with businesses outside Pakistan?
Yes. FORMIX works with clients anywhere — all communication and delivery happens remotely over WhatsApp and email.
Will my website work well on mobile?
Every FORMIX website is designed mobile-first and tested across phones, tablets and desktops before launch.
What do I need to get started?
Just a clear idea of your business and goals. Send us a message on the contact form or WhatsApp and we'll take it from there.
Do you offer support after launch?
Yes — maintenance and support are available so your website stays fast, secure and up to date.
